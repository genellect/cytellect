**Nuclear eccentricity did not change consistently with ActD 1 µM.**

We quantified nuclear eccentricity in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 51 (Untreated) and 59 (ActD 1 µM) nuclei from 9 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Per-run differences (ActD 1 µM − Untreated): −0.037 (Rep1), +0.000063 (Rep2) and +0.0015 (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the independence of the runs was not confirmed. Not shown: 48 nuclei without a value for this measure (listed in the source tables).
