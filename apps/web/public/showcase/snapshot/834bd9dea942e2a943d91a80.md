**Nuclear NPM1 integrated intensity increased by 0.96–100% with Oxaliplatin 10 µM.**

We quantified nuclear NPM1 integrated intensity in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei, coloured by run; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; boxes show the median and interquartile range of nuclei, whiskers the most extreme values within 1.5 × the interquartile range; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 86 (Untreated) and 65 (Oxaliplatin 10 µM) nuclei from 9 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Per-run changes of Oxaliplatin 10 µM relative to Untreated: +25% (Rep1), +104% (Rep2) and +1.0% (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the independence of the runs was not confirmed.
