**Nucleoplasmic NPM1 integrated intensity increased by 180,000–2,900,000 a.u. × pixel with Oxaliplatin 10 µM.**

We quantified nucleoplasmic NPM1 integrated intensity (background-subtracted) in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei, coloured by run; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 84 (Untreated) and 65 (Oxaliplatin 10 µM) nuclei from 9 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Per-run differences (Oxaliplatin 10 µM − Untreated): +1,733,060 (Rep1), +2,888,097 (Rep2) and +183,423 (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the independence of the runs was not confirmed.
