**Nuclear 5-EU mean intensity increased by 0.023–9.8% with ActD 1 µM.**

We quantified nuclear 5-EU mean intensity in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 44 (Untreated) and 30 (ActD 1 µM) nuclei from 7 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Only 5-EU-positive nuclei were analysed. Classification used Otsu thresholding per acquisition date of mean 5-EU intensities; a value strictly above the threshold was positive and equality was negative. Unclassified objects were not selected. Per-run changes of ActD 1 µM relative to Untreated: +6.5% (Rep1), +9.8% (Rep2) and +0.0% (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the positivity threshold was not set from a negative control.
