**Nucleolar area per nucleus decreased by 17–46% with Oxaliplatin 10 µM.**

Nucleolar area per nucleus in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Per-run changes of Oxaliplatin 10 µM relative to Untreated: −17% (Rep1), −17% (Rep2) and −46% (Rep3).
