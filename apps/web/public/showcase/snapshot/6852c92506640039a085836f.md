**Nucleolar area fraction of nucleus decreased by 7.8–12 percentage points with ActD 1 µM.**

We quantified nucleolar area fraction of nucleus in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei, coloured by run; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 84 (Untreated) and 71 (ActD 1 µM) nuclei from 9 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Per-run differences (ActD 1 µM − Untreated): −7.8 pp (Rep1), −8.2 pp (Rep2) and −12 pp (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the independence of the runs was not confirmed. Not shown: 3 nuclei without a value for this measure (listed in the source tables).
