**Nucleolar area fraction of nucleus decreased by 0.96–7.3 percentage points with Oxaliplatin 10 µM.**

We quantified nucleolar area fraction of nucleus in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 84 (Untreated) and 65 (Oxaliplatin 10 µM) nuclei from 9 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Per-run differences (Oxaliplatin 10 µM − Untreated): −2.9 pp (Rep1), −0.96 pp (Rep2) and −7.3 pp (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the independence of the runs was not confirmed. Not shown: 2 nuclei without a value for this measure (listed in the source tables).
