**Nuclear Hoechst mean intensity increased by 27–73 a.u. with PLK1 KO.**

We quantified nuclear Hoechst mean intensity (background-subtracted) in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei, coloured by run; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 3,762 (Non-targeting) and 670 (PLK1 KO) nuclei from 9 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Per-run differences (PLK1 KO − Non-targeting): +72 (Run1), +27 (Run2) and +73 (Run3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the independence of the runs was not confirmed.
