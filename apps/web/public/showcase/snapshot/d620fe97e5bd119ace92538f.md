**5-EU-positive nuclei per field did not change consistently with ActD 1 µM.**

We quantified 5-EU-positive nuclei per field in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual fields, coloured by run; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields); lines join the same run; bars show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 9 fields per condition; individual fields are not treated as independent replicates. 5-EU-positive nuclei were counted in each field. Classification used Otsu thresholding per acquisition date of mean 5-EU intensities; a value strictly above the threshold was positive and equality was negative. Unclassified objects were not selected. Per-run changes of ActD 1 µM relative to Untreated: +0.0% (Rep1), −24% (Rep2) and −81% (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the positivity threshold was not set from a negative control.
