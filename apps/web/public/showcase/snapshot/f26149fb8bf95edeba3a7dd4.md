**Nucleoplasmic NPM1 median intensity increased by 21–46% with ActD 1 µM.**

We quantified nucleoplasmic NPM1 median intensity in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei, coloured by run; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; bars show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 84 (Untreated) and 71 (ActD 1 µM) nuclei from 9 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Per-run changes of ActD 1 µM relative to Untreated: +21% (Rep1), +46% (Rep2) and +21% (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the independence of the runs was not confirmed.
