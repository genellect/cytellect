**Nuclear solidity decreased by 0.0096–0.014 with PLK1 KO.**

We quantified nuclear solidity in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei, coloured by run; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; boxes show the median and interquartile range of nuclei, whiskers the most extreme values within 1.5 × the interquartile range; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 3,404 (Non-targeting) and 466 (PLK1 KO) nuclei from 9 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Per-run differences (PLK1 KO − Non-targeting): −0.0096 (Run1), −0.014 (Run2) and −0.012 (Run3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the independence of the runs was not confirmed. Not shown: 562 nuclei without a value for this measure (listed in the source tables).
