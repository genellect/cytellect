**Nuclear NPM1 mean intensity did not change consistently with ActD 1 µM.**

We quantified nuclear NPM1 mean intensity in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei, coloured by run; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; boxes show the median and interquartile range of nuclei, whiskers the most extreme values within 1.5 × the interquartile range; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 86 (Untreated) and 72 (ActD 1 µM) nuclei from 9 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Per-run changes of ActD 1 µM relative to Untreated: +13% (Rep1), +51% (Rep2) and −9.4% (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the independence of the runs was not confirmed.
