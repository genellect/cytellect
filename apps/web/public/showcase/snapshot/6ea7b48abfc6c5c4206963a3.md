**Nuclear 5-EU mean intensity decreased by 39–300 a.u. with ActD 1 µM.**

We quantified nuclear 5-EU mean intensity (background-subtracted) in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei, coloured by run; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; boxes show the median and interquartile range of nuclei, whiskers the most extreme values within 1.5 × the interquartile range; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 42 (Untreated) and 42 (ActD 1 µM) nuclei from 8 (Untreated) and 7 (ActD 1 µM) fields; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Only 5-EU-negative nuclei were analysed. Classification used Otsu thresholding per acquisition date of mean 5-EU intensities; a value strictly above the threshold was positive and equality was negative. Unclassified objects were not selected. Per-run differences (ActD 1 µM − Untreated): −115 (Rep1), −300 (Rep2) and −39 (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the positivity threshold was not set from a negative control.
