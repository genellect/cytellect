**Nuclear NPM1 mean intensity did not change consistently with Cisplatin 10 µM.**

We quantified nuclear NPM1 mean intensity (background-subtracted) in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei, coloured by run; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 86 (Untreated) and 58 (Cisplatin 10 µM) nuclei from 9 (Untreated) and 8 (Cisplatin 10 µM) fields; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Only 5-EU-negative nuclei were analysed. Classification used Otsu thresholding per acquisition date of mean 5-EU intensities; a value strictly above the threshold was positive and equality was negative. Unclassified objects were not selected. Per-run differences (Cisplatin 10 µM − Untreated): +441 (Rep1), −453 (Rep2) and −273 (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the positivity threshold was not set from a negative control.
