**Nuclear Hoechst integrated intensity decreased by 6.1–32% with PLK1 KO.**

We quantified nuclear Hoechst integrated intensity in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; bars show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 3,762 (Non-targeting) and 670 (PLK1 KO) nuclei from 9 fields per condition; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Per-run changes of PLK1 KO relative to Non-targeting: −26% (Run1), −32% (Run2) and −6.1% (Run3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the independence of the runs was not confirmed.
