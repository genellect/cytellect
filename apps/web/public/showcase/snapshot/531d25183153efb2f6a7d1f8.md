**Nuclear 5-EU mean intensity increased by 1.3–45% with Oxaliplatin 10 µM.**

We quantified nuclear 5-EU mean intensity in [Cell line] cells ([treatment and duration]) labelled with [antibody or dye for each stain]. Small dots show individual nuclei; large symbols show the value of each run (the mean of its fields, each field summarised by the median of its nuclei); lines join the same run; horizontal lines show the mean of runs. n = 3 runs per condition; 41 (Untreated) and 35 (Oxaliplatin 10 µM) nuclei from 7 (Untreated) and 9 (Oxaliplatin 10 µM) fields; individual nuclei and fields are not treated as independent replicates. Only 5-EU-positive nuclei were analysed. Classification used Otsu thresholding per acquisition date of mean 5-EU intensities; a value strictly above the threshold was positive and equality was negative. Unclassified objects were not selected. Per-run changes of Oxaliplatin 10 µM relative to Untreated: +1.3% (Rep1), +45% (Rep2) and +5.6% (Rep3). No hypothesis test or confidence interval was calculated because the positivity threshold was not set from a negative control.
